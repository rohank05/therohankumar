## Platform

## Stack

Next.js 14 (app router), React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind installed but the site is styled with a hand-written global stylesheet. Deployed at https://therohankumar.com.

## Purpose

Personal portfolio of Rohan Kumar, a software engineer (SDE 1 at NovoStack, 3+ years) building full-stack systems across CRM, fintech and IoT. Node, NestJS, Go backends; React / Next.js frontends; AWS, Postgres, CI infra.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers skimming many portfolios. Success is that they remember Rohan and act: email mail@therohankumar.com or download the resume (/Rohan_Kumar_Resume.pdf).

## Content that must be preserved

All current content in `lib/data.ts` and the components: projects (Auto Analytics Pro, HelloTrade BRE, NovoHR, RetailSync, jikan-api.js, Beat Music), experience (NovoStack, Spraxa Solutions), skills, education and certifications, contact email, GitHub (rohank05), LinkedIn (rohank05). SEO metadata and OG/Twitter images in `app/`.

Microsoft Clarity analytics (`components/ClarityInit.tsx`) must stay wired in.

Not required to keep (owner's call, 2026-09-24): live iframe hover previews of projects; the phone number in the footer.

## Brand commitments

Owner wants the site loud, colourful and funky, never monotonous, and fine with pushing it "all the way" (playful shapes, big type, interactive toys) as long as it still reads as a serious engineer.

## Accessibility

Standard WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
